## Nature of the project

TreeGov is a workbench for credit-risk segmentation with native
MRM/IFRS 9 governance. It implements **classical decision-tree
algorithms** — CHAID (Kass, 1980), C&RT (Breiman et al., 1984),
QUEST (Loh & Shih, 1997) — with an emphasis on **auditability,
traceability, and white-box mathematics**, not on novel methodology.

It is developed by its author as an independent open-source
initiative.

It is released under the **Apache License 2.0** (see `LICENSE`).

It is **not**, at this stage, fit for production use in any financial
institution (see the regulatory notice in `README.md`).
