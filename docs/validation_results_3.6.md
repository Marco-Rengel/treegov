### 2026-11-03 — Why car_evaluation (CRT/QUEST) has KS = 0.0

Multiclass KS is the minimum pairwise KS (decision D4 in
`docs/3.5_design_multiclass_weighted_reports.md` §2.3). With `max_depth=3` and
`min_child_size=50`, the tree cannot isolate the minority classes
`good` (~48 training rows) or `vgood` (~46 training rows): both fall into the
same leaves. The (good, vgood) pair produces `ks_pair = 0.0`, and because the
aggregate is the minimum, it drives the overall KS to 0.0. This does not
indicate a model failure: the Hand & Till AUC (0.7330) measures aggregate
performance and is the appropriate indicator for K>2. CHAID does separate that
pair (it has 9 leaves), which is why its KS is 0.4435.
